Free AI Tools — ChatGPT & Claude

ChatGPT and Claude are now free to all current UT students, faculty, and staff. Paid tiers are available if you need higher usage limits — see tier details on the AI Studio page linked below.
First-time login
Go to ChatGPT or Claude.
  1. Enter your UT EID in the login field using the format yourEID@eid.utexas.edu. Credentials must match this format exactly.
  2. Continue through the Microsoft login page.
  3. Enter your UT EID credentials on the University of Texas at Austin authentication page.
  4. Complete Duo multi-factor authentication if prompted.
  5. After authentication, you should be redirected into the UT Austin ChatGPT or Claude environment.
Verify your account
Click your name in the bottom left corner — it should show UT Austin. If not, sign out and sign back in with your UT EID email. Still having issues? Try clearing your browser cache. More info: UT AI Studio — AI Tools and Services.

Statistical & Analytic Support (Ascension)
The Ascension Data Science Institute (ADSI) offers statistical consulting for national-level research projects — exploratory analysis, descriptive and Bayesian statistics, regression, hierarchical modeling, survival analysis, propensity score matching, machine learning, and more. Research ideas need service-line/clinical-leader approval and must support Ascension's strategic priorities; once approved, complete the ADSI RedCap research submission form and a team member will follow up on scope and timeline. Contact allison.ottenbacher@ascension.org.
Between Ascension and UT, there's a real bench of support available for QI and research projects — statistical consulting, translational research infrastructure, and data science collaboration all listed below. Don't overlook the librarians on either side, either: at both Ascension and UT, they are often incredibly helpful in tracking down literature, running searches, and pointing you toward the right resource — take advantage of them.

Staff Tuition Assistance Program (STAP)
A reminder that STAP covers in-state tuition for one eligible course per semester (or multiple courses up to 3 combined credit hours) at UT Austin. To be eligible, you must be a full-time staff employee, have worked at UT for at least one continuous year, have a rating of Meets Expectations or higher on your most recent appraisal, be admitted to UT Austin as a degree or non-degree student, and be registered for classes. Note: graduate coursework covered by STAP is taxable; undergraduate is not. Full details & eligibility.

Milestones Survey
A quick survey to track your professional milestones this year — please complete it, even if this isn't your first time. Complete the survey.
Why I keep asking: this ACGME-adapted milestone-tool pilot (presented as a poster this year at the Consortium of Advanced Practice Providers) paired self- and preceptor-ratings on 30 competency items, scored 1 (novice) to 4 (independent/expert), at multiple points in orientation. The findings suggest this kind of milestone tracking is genuinely useful as an adjunct measure of APP development — not a replacement for narrative feedback, but a complementary data point that can show progression (or a plateau) more objectively than impressions alone.
That's also why I want each of you completing this annually for your first 4–5 years rather than just once: a single score only tells you how things turned out. A trend line — recorded every year through the stretch where most APPs move from closely-supervised to fully independent practice — is what actually shows whether growth is on track, has plateaued, or needs a different kind of support, early enough to do something about it. It's the same logic as the pilot, just stretched across a career instead of a single orientation.
