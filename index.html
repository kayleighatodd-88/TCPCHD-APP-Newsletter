Call for Abstracts — PC4/PAC3 Spring 2027 Poster Session

Poster Session: April 7, 2027 · Sheraton Pittsburgh Hotel at Station Square, Pittsburgh, PA · hosted by UPMC Children's Hospital of Pittsburgh.

The program committee invites abstracts on standards of care, mortality, length of stay, complications, patient/family experience, registry data processes, and QI or patient safety work. Projects can be in the design phase or have early results, and previously presented work is welcome. QI projects should highlight at least one QI tool (key driver diagram, PDSA cycle, Pareto chart or SPC chart). This is a great home for your QI project!

  • 500-word limit with Introduction/Objective, Method, Results and Conclusion
  • One submission per primary author
